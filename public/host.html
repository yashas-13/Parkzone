Safety

A renter never gets your desktop.

This is the part that decides whether hosting is sane or terrifying. On ParkZone, renter code runs inside a Docker container with hard limits, and you hold the kill switch the whole time.

Container isolation

Workloads execute inside Docker, never on Windows directly. No filesystem, registry or browser access to your machine.

Resource caps

Memory, CPU and process limits are applied to every container so one job cannot wedge your PC.

Approved images only

Renters pick from an allow-list of well-known images. Arbitrary images and mining payloads are rejected by the API.

Your kill switch

Close the agent or pull the network cable. Running instances stop billing and the listing leaves the marketplace within two minutes.

Your hours, your rules

Only advertise while you are asleep, at work, or gaming-free. Nothing is rented outside the window you allow.

No exclusivity

No contract, no lock-in, no penalty for switching off. Delete the agent and you are out.
